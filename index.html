Facilities

Everything a match day needs

Built for five-a-side football and box cricket, and kept ready for the casual kickabout too. Floodlit, monitored and cleaned between every booking.

FIFA-grade synthetic turf

Even-pile synthetic grass over a shock-pad base, so the ball rolls true and studs bite without giving way. Marked for both football and box cricket.

Two sports · one surface

Night floodlights

High-intensity floodlights with even coverage right to the corners, so a 9 PM game plays as cleanly as a 6 PM one.

Lit until 22:00

Changing rooms

Separate lockers and washrooms on site, cleaned daily before the first booking.

Free parking

Unmarked but secure parking on the gate side, enough for a full eleven-a-side plus spectators.

Water & refreshments

Drinking water and a small counter at the pavilion. Refill your bottles rather than buying plastic.

First aid on site

Padded boundary netting to keep stray balls in play, and a first-aid kit with staff on hand during peak hours.
